# USA NCLEX Pathway

## Requirements
- Valid RN License from home country
- NCLEX-RN Exam
- English Test: TOEFL or IELTS
- VisaScreen Certificate
- CGFNS Credential Evaluation
- State Board Application (varies by state)

## Timeline
12-24 months

## Cost Estimate
$5,000 - $10,000 USD

## Difficulty
High

## Best For
- Nurses seeking highest salaries
- Nurses with strong exam preparation
- Nurses willing to wait longer

## Key Facts
- NCSBN runs the NCLEX
- Each US state has its own board of nursing
- Nurses earn $70,000 - $120,000/year
- Green card sponsorship often available
