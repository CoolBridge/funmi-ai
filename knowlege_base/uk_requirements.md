# UK NMC Pathway

## Requirements
- Valid RN License
- CBT Exam (Computer Based Test)
- OSCE Exam (Objective Structured Clinical Examination)
- English Test: IELTS (7.0+) or OET (Grade B)
- Good Standing Certificate from home country

## Timeline
6-12 months

## Cost Estimate
$3,000 - $5,000 USD

## Difficulty
Medium

## Best For
- Nurses with strong clinical background
- Nurses with good English
- African/Asian nurses (high demand)

## Key Facts
- NMC is the regulatory body
- CBT is done online from home country
- OSCE is done in the UK
- High nurse shortage = fast processing
