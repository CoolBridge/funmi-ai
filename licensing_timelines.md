# Licensing Timelines by Country

## United Kingdom
- CBT Preparation: 1-3 months
- CBT Exam + Results: 1 month
- NMC Application: 1-2 months
- OSCE Preparation: 2-3 months
- OSCE Exam + Results: 1 month
- Total: 6-12 months

## United States
- CGFNS Evaluation: 3-6 months
- State Board Application: 2-4 months
- NCLEX Preparation: 2-4 months
- NCLEX Exam + Results: 1 month
- VisaScreen: 3-6 months
- Total: 12-24 months

## Canada
- NNAS Assessment: 3-6 months
- Provincial Application: 2-3 months
- NCLEX Preparation: 2-4 months
- NCLEX Exam + Results: 1 month
- Total: 12-18 months

## Fastest to Slowest
1. UK (6-12 months)
2. Canada (12-18 months)
3. USA (12-24 months)
