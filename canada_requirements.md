# Canada NCLEX-RN Pathway

## Requirements
- Valid RN License from home country
- NCLEX-RN Exam (since 2015)
- English Test: IELTS or CELBAN
- Credential Evaluation (NNAS)
- Provincial Registration (varies by province)

## Timeline
12-18 months

## Cost Estimate
$4,000 - $7,000 USD

## Difficulty
Medium-High

## Best For
- Nurses who want PR pathway
- Nurses with family in Canada
- Nurses who prefer cold climate countries

## Key Facts
- NNAS does the credential assessment
- Ontario and British Columbia are top destinations
- Express Entry pathway available
- Nurses earn CAD $65,000 - $95,000/year
