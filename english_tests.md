# English Language Tests for Nurses

## IELTS Academic
- Required Score: 7.0 overall (no band below 6.5)
- Used For: UK, Canada, Australia
- Cost: ~$250 USD
- Valid For: 2 years
- Tip: Most common test, widely accepted

## OET (Occupational English Test)
- Required Score: Grade B in all sections
- Used For: UK, Australia, New Zealand
- Cost: ~$587 USD
- Valid For: 2 years
- Tip: Healthcare-specific, easier for nurses

## TOEFL iBT
- Required Score: 83+ overall
- Used For: USA, Canada
- Cost: ~$220 USD
- Valid For: 2 years
- Tip: Computer-based, academic focus

## CELBAN
- Required Score: varies by province
- Used For: Canada only
- Cost: ~$600 CAD
- Valid For: 2 years
- Tip: Healthcare-specific for Canada
